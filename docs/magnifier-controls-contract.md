On the focused Magnifier page, exact, non-repeating Alt+B toggles the selected user's staged lens-border setting, and Alt+U toggles staged automatic refresh. The keys are suppressed when the page is unfocused, text editing or an open popup claims keyboard input, or work is busy/pending. They change the draft only. Neither access-key receipts nor configuration ACKs establish magnifier image pixels or capture correctness.
